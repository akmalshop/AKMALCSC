Aksar Pooche Jane Wale Sawal

Punjab National Bank ka khata kholne ke liye kya chahiye? +
PNB Kiosk CSP par naya savings ya Jan-Dhan khata kholne ke liye aapka Aadhaar Card, PAN card (ya Form 60), 2 passport size photo aur mobile number zaroori hai. Khata turant activate ho jata hai.
Kya dusre banks ka paisa bhi anguthe se nikal sakte hain? +
Haan! Hamare PNB CSP Center par AEPS ke dwara State Bank (SBI), Bank of Baroda (BoB), Union Bank, Canara Bank aur sabhi gramin banko ka paisa angutha lagakar nikala aur check kiya jata hai.
Mujhe kaam karwane ke liye cafe aana padega ya online ho jayega? +
Zyadatar kaam jaise PAN Card, PF Claim, Ration Card, Admit Card aur Form Filling aap ghar baithe WhatsApp par documents bhejkar karwa sakte hain. Biometric fingerprint wale kaamo ke liye aap lounge par visit kar sakte hain.
PVC Smart Card kitni der me ready hota hai? +
Agar aapke paas PDF available hai to cafe aane ke sirf 5 se 10 minute ke andar high-grade PVC Smart Card print ho kar haath me mil jata hai.
